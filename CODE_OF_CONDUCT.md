# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting comments, public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Community leaders will remove contributions that are not aligned with this code and may ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate. Report concerns to conduct@younique.dev.
